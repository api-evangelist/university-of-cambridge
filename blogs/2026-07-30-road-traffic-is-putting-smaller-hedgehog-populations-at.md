Road traffic is putting smaller hedgehog populations at risk of collapse Jacqueline Garget Fri, 07/31/2026 - 00:01 A new study involving the University of Cambridge has found that road traffic kills between 15-29% of hedgehogs in local populations each year. The study, funded by The British Hedgehog Preservation Society and People’s Trust for Endangered Species, investigated how road traffic deaths impact local hedgehog populations of different sizes in the short and longer term.
