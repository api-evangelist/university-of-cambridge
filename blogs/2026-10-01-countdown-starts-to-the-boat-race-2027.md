Countdown starts to The Boat Race 2027 Hilary Fletcher Thu, 10/01/2026 - 15:28 This season has added significance as 2027 is the centenary of the Women’s Race. To mark the anniversary, two new trophies will be awarded to the victorious men’s and women’s crews. As well as this, the order of the races on Sunday 11 April 2027 will be reversed.
