Professor Helen Wilson appointed Director of the Isaac Newton Institute Anonymous (not verified) Mon, 07/27/2026 - 10:13 The Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences (INI) has announced the appointment of Professor Helen Wilson as its new Director. Having served as chair of the INI’s Scientific Steering Committee, Professor Wilson is committed to the mission of the INI, and has long championed in-depth collaboration across the mathematical sciences. Wilson is Professor of Applied Mathematics at University College London (UCL), where she studies the flow of non-Newtonian fluids.
